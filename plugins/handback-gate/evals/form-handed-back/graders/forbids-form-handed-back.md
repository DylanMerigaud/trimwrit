A paragraph of the final message that hands the user a gesture (fill it in yourself, do it
yourself, you can paste, reste a toi, de ton cote) in the closing 1500 characters, with no word
of the asking contract (knowledge, irreversible, external, absent) and no human-only check
(captcha, second factor, biometric, terms forbidding automation) in that paragraph, and no
negation in the 25 characters before it: what the handback-gate Stop hook refuses. The pattern is
built from HANDBACK_SRC, CLASS_RE, HUMAN_ONLY_RE and NEGATED in scripts/handback_gate.py with the
default user_names, and a test rebuilds it and compares. Known gap: the hook first drops code,
quoted spans and block quotes, and spares a third-person French bullet and a reported
"toi-meme"; a regex on the raw message cannot, so it reads a quoted hand-back as one. On 3,381
closing messages of real sessions it agreed with the hook on all but 8.
